Preparing an M&A deal: four questions to settle first

01.11.2026 · Sample article (mockup)

Before negotiating price in depth, companies should align on four points: strategic goal, diligence scope (legal / financial / operations), report deliverables, and post-signing responsibilities.

1. What is the deal goal?

Market expansion, technology add-on, or ownership restructuring? Goals drive evaluation criteria.

2. How far does diligence go?

Early alignment prevents mid-stream scope creep and keeps filing timelines intact.

3. What outputs are required?

Strengths / weaknesses report, transaction opinion, or procedure dossiers — each output needs a clear milestone.

4. Who owns post-deal work?

Operating stabilization is often overlooked. Capture post-deal support needs up front.
