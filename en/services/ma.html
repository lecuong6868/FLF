Mergers & acquisitions advisory

Sample detail page — reference content with cautious wording.

Customer

Fintech and SME companies on the buy or sell side that need end-to-end transaction support.

Common problems

No suitable target yet; missing evaluation criteria; multi-step filings; post-deal operating stability.

Scope of work

  • Pre-deal: target search (if needed), general process advice, legal / asset / financial assessment by client role.
  • Execution: prepare dossiers; represent the client with authorities under authorization.
  • Post-deal: stabilize operations; monthly advisory if requested.

Deliverables

  • Strengths / weaknesses analysis based on available records.
  • Professional opinion for the specific transaction plan.
  • Procedure dossier pack and licensing / tax status updates when in scope.

Specialized legal items may be coordinated with partner law practices. This mockup does not claim unverified capabilities.
