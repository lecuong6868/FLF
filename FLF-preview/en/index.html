01 — About

FLF works around needs, scope, and deliverables

First Legal Force (FLF) is a multi-disciplinary business advisory firm. We focus on understanding your situation, separating what can start now from what needs extra coordination — before proposing a roadmap. When work falls under the practice of law or specialised legal fields, FLF collaborates with partner law firms under terms agreed with the client.

Client needs

Clarify the operating, expansion, or compliance challenge you face — in plain language, not a rigid service catalogue.

Scope of work

Agree what FLF owns and what other parties handle (including legal partners) before work begins, to avoid overlap or gaps.

Expected outcomes

Each step ties to a concrete deliverable: a needs brief, a roadmap, a checklist, or a named contact — so you know what you are receiving.

02 — Practice areas

Three groups of support under review

Business & governance advisory

Who it is for: companies that need to clarify operating or governance issues before engaging a specialist firm.
Common friction: unclear starting point; processes that depend on one person; no coordinating contact.
FLF scope: clarify needs, frame the work, coordinate across disciplines at an advisory level.
Outcomes: needs summary, proposed roadmap, named owner.

Investment & expansion

Who it is for: businesses or investors considering expansion, partnerships, or operating structures in Vietnam.
Common friction: unclear sequence between advisory work and legal procedures.
FLF scope: step orientation, needs checklist, partner coordination when work requires licensed legal practice.
Outcomes: step roadmap and a list of items that need a legal partner (if any).

Compliance & licensing Proposed

Who it is for: companies that need a directional review of obligations or licences.
Common friction: unclear which licences are mandatory; worry about the wrong sequence.
FLF scope: directional advisory review and a work list — this Preview does not promise licence issuance.
Outcomes: related work list and FLF / partner split.

03 — Process

Four steps from intake to delivery

  1. 01

    Intake

    You share context through the form or public contact channels.

  2. 02

    Clarify needs

    We discuss the problem to solve, FLF’s scope, and where a legal partner may be required.

  3. 03

    Propose roadmap & outcomes

    Align on the working plan, deliverables per step, owners, and priorities.

  4. 04

    Deliver within the agreed scope

    Execute as agreed; share progress updates; do not expand scope without a new alignment.
