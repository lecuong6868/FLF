01 — Who we are

A multi-disciplinary business advisory firm

First Legal Force (FLF) is a multi-disciplinary business advisory firm. We work with companies that need to clarify the situation they face, decide what to prioritise, and agree what they will receive after each step — before expanding scope or engaging extra specialists.

We do not start from a rigid service catalogue. We start from client needs: operations, expansion, compliance, or coordinating several workstreams at once. FLF helps separate what can begin now from what needs further coordination, then proposes a roadmap tied to concrete deliverables.

When work falls under the practice of law or specialised legal fields, FLF collaborates with partner law firms under terms agreed with the client. This Preview deliberately does not name partners until the client approves public listing.

02 — How we intake challenges

From initial context to a clear work frame

Intake aims to help both sides share the same picture: the problem to solve, what information is still missing, and which parts are business advisory versus other parties.

  1. 01

    Receive context

    You share a short situation description through the form or public channels: what you face, what you want to achieve, and when you need a response.

  2. 02

    Clarify the key questions

    FLF asks follow-ups to separate symptoms from causes, and to distinguish operating / governance work from specialised legal procedures (if any).

  3. 03

    Lock scope before delivery

    Both sides agree what FLF owns, what you handle, and what partners cover — plus the outcomes expected at each step.

03 — Scope of work

What FLF focuses on

Within advisory scope

Clarifying needs; framing work and priorities; coordinating across disciplines at an advisory level; building checklists / roadmaps; naming owners for agreed workstreams.

Outside the default scope

Licensed legal practice, litigation representation, or other regulated procedures — those parts are connected to suitable partners when the client agrees.

Changing scope

If new work appears mid-engagement, FLF does not expand scope unilaterally: both sides realign before extra delivery starts.

Practice-area groups under review on the home page (business & governance advisory; investment & expansion; compliance & licensing — proposed) will be confirmed by the client before dedicated pages are built. See practice areas on the home page

04 — Deliverables

Every step ties to something you can review

This Preview does not promise unverified business or legal outcomes. Below are the kinds of advisory deliverables FLF wants the new site to make explicit — so you know what you receive at each stage.

Needs summary

A shared record of the agreed challenge, open assumptions, and information still needed — before work continues.

Roadmap & checklist

Sequence of work, FLF / client / partner split, and review points when scope may need revisiting.

Named owners

Who tracks each agreed workstream, and how progress updates are shared within the agreed scope.
